1. What Paidly is

Paidly is not a narrow invoicing app. It is a financial workflow platform for small and medium businesses: one place to issue commercial documents, manage clients and catalog, collect payment, see cash performance, and grow through channels such as affiliates.

Mission: Help businesses get paid faster by reducing friction between sending a document and receiving money.

Investor framing: From “another invoice PDF tool” to owning the money loop — Document → Payment Intent → Settlement → Intelligence.

2. The problem we solve

Typical toolGap
Invoicing-onlyWeak relationships, weak cash intelligence, bolted-on payments
Accounting-onlyHeavy; slow path from “quote sent” to “money in”
Standalone CRM / referralDisconnected from documents and payment state

Paidly’s edge: unify issuance, relationships/catalog, revenue/ops, and payment intelligence under one architecture — so the business runs in one system, not five tabs.

3. How the product operates

Create document (invoice / quote / payslip) ↓ Compose (line items, tax, branding) ↓ Deliver (email, public link, client portal) ↓ Observe (opens, clicks, reminders) ↓ Payment intent → PayFast / rails ↓ Settle (paid / accepted / archived) ↓ Intelligence (smart reminders, at-risk follow-ups)

Day-to-day for owners

  1. Add clients and catalog
  2. Create branded invoices, quotes, payslips
  3. Send via email or shareable link; track views
  4. Collect payment (PayFast); record partials
  5. Use dashboard, cash flow, and reports
  6. Grow via affiliates and team workspaces

Architecture maturity

Roughly ~70% of the SaaS operating system is in place. The remaining ~30% that defines long-term defensibility: payment abstraction at scale, unified document event intelligence, and experience consistency across every money surface.

7. Competitive narrative

Old lens: “We have invoices, quotes, clients…”
New lens: One Document Engine, one money loop, one Get Paid intelligence layer.

Competitors typically win one vertical. Paidly’s thesis is workflow ownership: issue → know who bought what → collect safely → remind intelligently → grow via affiliates → operate the company with roles and subscriptions.

That stack is the defensible story for shareholders: not feature count, but system coherence.

8. Near-term priorities

  1. Deepen Document Engine unification — one compose/send/PDF model across document kinds
  2. Client Timeline — full history per client
  3. Quote → accepted → draft invoice — close the sales-to-AR loop
  4. First-class Payment Intent — multi-provider path + consistent Pay / Retry / Receipt UX
  5. Payment Intelligence v1 — e.g. viewed 3× unpaid → reminder + follow-up
  6. Foundation (parallel) — auth/session hardening + UI layout standardization

9. Business model

  • SaaS subscription; plans catalog-driven (not hardcoded in UI)
  • Subscriptions activate only after verified PayFast ITN — frontend cannot fake “paid”
  • Append-only payment history and event timeline for auditability
  • Affiliate channel for acquisition without a separate growth product
